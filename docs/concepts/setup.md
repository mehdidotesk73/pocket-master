# Setup

## Record lines

These are the things each new record asks for. Each line has a kind:

- **Bank account**: adds
- **Money coming in**: adds (for example a reimbursement)
- **Credit card**: subtracts your share (set the default share % here)
- **Bill due**: subtracts (a bill due this month that isn't paid yet)
- **Money going out**: subtracts (for example paying someone back)

Rename, reorder, add or remove lines, then tap **Save changes**. Records you've already saved keep their own copy and aren't affected.

## Your data

- **Export all** makes a backup (.zip). On iPhone, choose **Save to Files** in the share sheet.
- **Import** restores a backup. You can **merge** it with what's here, where records on the same date are replaced, or **replace everything**. It also accepts a JSON export from the earlier prototype.
- **Protected from clean-up** shows whether the browser has agreed not to delete your data when space runs low. The installed app usually says Yes.
