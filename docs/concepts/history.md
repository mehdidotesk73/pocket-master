# History

Every record you've saved, oldest to newest.

- The **chart** shows left so far (with a dot for each record), plus assets and liabilities as thin lines. Tap or hover to read values.
- The **table** lists records newest first. To delete one, tap ✕ and then **Delete?** to confirm.
- To correct a record, open **+ New record** on the Today tab, pick that record's date, and save. It replaces the old one.

Changing your lines in Setup never changes saved records. Each record keeps its own copy of the line names and amounts.
