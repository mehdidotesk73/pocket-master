# Future

The projection starts from your **latest record** and walks forward one day at a time:

- **Every month** items (paychecks, rent, subscriptions) are added or taken away on their day. Day 31 means the last day of the month.
- **One-time** items are applied on their date.
- **Everyday spending** is taken away a little each day.

The heading tells you the main finding. If left so far goes below zero, it says when.

## "In record"

Some monthly items already appear as a line in your records until they're paid, like rent listed as a bill. Tick **in record** for those. The projection then skips them in your latest record's month, because that record already counts them, and applies them from the next month on. Paychecks usually stay unticked.

## Everyday spending

This is money that goes out beyond your scheduled items: groceries, gas, eating out.

- **Use my average**: once your records span four weeks or more, the app compares how left so far actually moved between records with what your scheduled items explain. The rest is everyday spending. It uses records from the last 120 days.
- **Set it myself**: use a fixed monthly amount. The same amount is used until your average is available.

## Saving

Edits are a draft. The chart updates as you go, and nothing is kept until you tap **Save changes**. **Discard** puts everything back.

This is a guide, not a promise. It's only as good as the items you've entered.
