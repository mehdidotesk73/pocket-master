# Past

Every record you've saved, oldest to newest.

- The **chart card** has four views:
  - **Totals**: **net equity** as a white line with a dot for each record. **Total owned** (blue) and **total owed** (red) are a dot per record, without lines. Net equity is owned minus owed, so it sits below each blue dot by the height of the red one. Tap a record to read all three.
  - **Timeseries**: every line of your records (checking, saving, each card) over time. Tap a name to hide or show it.
  - **Averages**: each line's **average** over the records you pick (all, the past N days, the past N records, or between two dates), as a donut with its share of the total. Choose **Owned**, **Owed**, or **Owned + owed** (both added as sizes), and tap names to leave lines out.
  - **Rates**: one **candle per day** for a line (or net equity), from the day's opening value to its closing value: green if it went up, red if it went down, with a thin wick to the day's high and low. Between two records the value is spread evenly at the rate between them, so those days are small, lighter candles of that average daily change. On a day with records, the candle shows the actual jumps, e.g. a card going from ($1,800) at 8 AM to $0 after you pay it. **Rising** and **Falling** show just one kind. Tap a candle to read the day, its records, or the average rate it sits in. For a card, going up means you owe less.
- The **table** lists records newest first, with the time each was taken. Several on the same day each get their own row.
- **Tap a record** to open it: change its amounts, date or time and tap **Update record**, or **Delete** it. ✕ in the table deletes too (tap again to confirm).

Changing the record template never changes saved records. Each record keeps its own copy of its rows.
