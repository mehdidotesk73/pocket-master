# Past

Every record you've saved, oldest to newest.

- The **chart card** has four views:
  - **Totals**: **net equity** as a white line with a dot for each record. **Total owned** (blue) and **total owed** (red) are a dot per record, without lines. Net equity is owned minus owed, so it sits below each blue dot by the height of the red one. Tap a record to read all three.
  - **Timeseries**: every line of your records (checking, saving, each card) over time. Tap a name to hide or show it.
  - **Averages**: each line's **average** over the records you pick (all, the past N days, the past N records, or between two dates), as a donut. Tap a line in the list to leave it out or bring it back. The middle adds up the lines left in: with every line in, that's your average net equity; with just the cards, your average owed. Each slice is that line's share of their size.
  - **Rates**: one **candle per day** for a line (or net equity), from the previous day's close to this day's: green if it went up, red if it went down. A day with records closes at its **last** record, and the day's records show as a thin wick. For example, a payment day dips to ($1,800) at 8 AM and closes at $0 after you pay. Between record days the change is spread evenly, so those days are small, lighter candles of that average daily change. It runs by date, not time of day, so a before/after pair that didn't change makes a flat day. **Rising** and **Falling** show just one kind. Tap a candle to read the day, its records, or the average rate it sits in. For a card, going up means you owe less.
- **Project this** (under Averages and Rates) turns what you're looking at into a forecast projection that keeps up with your records: a line's **average**, or its **daily change** (every day, or only rising or falling days), over the records you pick, repeating every day, week or month. Pick the forecast to add it to; **See it in Future** takes you there. See the Future help page for how it behaves.
- The **table** lists records newest first, with the time each was taken. Several on the same day each get their own row.
- **Tap a record** to open it: change its amounts, date or time and tap **Update record**, or **Delete** it. ✕ in the table deletes too (tap again to confirm).

Changing the record template never changes saved records. Each record keeps its own copy of its rows.
