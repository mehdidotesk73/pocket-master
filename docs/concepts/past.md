# Past

Every record you've saved, oldest to newest.

- The **chart** shows **net equity** as a white line with a dot for each record. **Total owned** (blue) and **total owed** (red) are a dot per record, without lines. Net equity is owned minus owed, so it sits below each blue dot by the height of the red one. Tap or hover a record to read all three.
- The **table** lists records newest first, with the time each was taken. Several on the same day each get their own row.
- **Tap a record** to open it: change its amounts, date or time and tap **Update record**, or **Delete** it. ✕ in the table deletes too (tap again to confirm).

Changing the record template never changes saved records. Each record keeps its own copy of its rows.
