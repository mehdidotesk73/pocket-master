# Past

Every record you've saved, oldest to newest.

- The **chart card** has four views:
  - **Totals**: **net equity** as a white line with a dot for each record. **Total owned** (blue) and **total owed** (red) are a dot per record, without lines. Net equity is owned minus owed, so it sits below each blue dot by the height of the red one. Tap a record to read all three.
  - **Each line**: every line of your records (checking, saving, each card) over time. Tap a name to hide or show it.
  - **Mix**: each line's **average** over the records you pick (all, the past N days, the past N records, or between two dates), as a donut with its share of the total. Choose **Owned**, **Owed**, or **Owned + owed** (both added as sizes), and tap names to leave lines out.
  - **Rate**: how fast a line (or net equity) changed **per day** between records. Each bar spans the days from one record to the next: above $0 it grew, below it shrank. **Growing** and **Shrinking** show just one kind. Records on the same day count as that day's last one, so paying a card doesn't make a spike. For a card, a bar below $0 means you owed more each day.
- The **table** lists records newest first, with the time each was taken. Several on the same day each get their own row.
- **Tap a record** to open it: change its amounts, date or time and tap **Update record**, or **Delete** it. ✕ in the table deletes too (tap again to confirm).

Changing the record template never changes saved records. Each record keeps its own copy of its rows.
