# Today

## Left so far

**Left so far = assets − liabilities.**

- **Assets**: bank accounts plus money coming in (for example a reimbursement you're owed).
- **Liabilities**: credit cards at your share, bills due this month that aren't paid yet, and money you've committed to pay out.

Type every amount as a positive number. Whether a line adds or subtracts depends on its kind, which you choose in Setup.

## Saving a record

Tap **+ New record**. Every field starts with the value from your latest record, so you only change what moved. The totals at the bottom update as you type.

- **One record per day.** If you pick a date that already has a record, the button says **Update record** and saving replaces it.
- **Cancel** (or ✕, or Esc) throws the draft away.

## Credit cards and "my share"

If you split a card with someone, enter the card's **total balance** and your **share %**. Only your share counts. The share starts at the default from Setup, and you can change it in any record.

## The figures

- **Bank balances**: your account lines added up.
- **Cards, my share**: what you owe on cards, counting only your share.
- **Projected in N days**: where the Projection tab says you'll be. If it dips lower than that on the way, the low point is shown too.
