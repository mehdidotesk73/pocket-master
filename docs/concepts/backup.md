# Export and Import

Your records live only on this device, inside this app. Open the menu (**☰**, top left), tap **Records**, then **Import** or **Export**.

## Export

- **Zip file**: everything as files (`records/<date>.json`, `projection.json`, `template.json`). On iPhone, choose **Save to Files** in the share sheet.
- **Copy to clipboard**: everything as one JSON text. Paste it into a note, a message or an email to keep it. It's limited to 500 KB so the paste doesn't stall your phone; if your data is bigger, use the zip file.

## Import

- **Zip or JSON file**: pick a zip you exported, or a JSON file (this app's or the earlier prototype's).
- **Paste from clipboard**: reads the copied JSON. iPhone asks you to tap **Paste** first. If the app can't read the clipboard, a box appears: paste the text into it and tap **Read it**.

Either way, you then choose **Merge**, which keeps your records and replaces any on the same dates, or **Replace everything**. Nothing changes until you choose.

**Protected from clean-up** shows whether the browser has agreed not to delete your data when space runs low. The installed app usually says Yes. The Present tab reminds you if you haven't exported in two weeks.
