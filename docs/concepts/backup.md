# Backup

Your records live only on this device, inside this app. Open **Backup** in the footer to manage them.

- **Export all** makes a backup (.zip). On iPhone, choose **Save to Files** in the share sheet.
- **Import** restores a backup. You can **merge** it with what's here, where records on the same date are replaced, or **replace everything**. It also accepts a JSON export from the earlier prototype.
- **Protected from clean-up** shows whether the browser has agreed not to delete your data when space runs low. The installed app usually says Yes.

The Present tab reminds you if you haven't backed up in two weeks.
