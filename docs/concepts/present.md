# Present

## Left so far

A record is a list of line items, each a name and an amount:

- **Positive** amounts are things you have: bank balances, money coming in.
- **Negative** amounts are things you owe or have committed: card balances, bills due, money going out.

The app sorts them for you: **left so far = what you have − what you owe**.

## Saving a record

Tap **+ New record**. You get a compact list of rows, already filled in from your latest record, so you only change what moved.

- Tap **+** / **−** at the start of an amount to switch a row between have and owe.
- **+ Add row** adds a line; **✕** removes one.
- **Save as template** makes the current rows the starting list for every new record from now on.
- **One record per day.** If you pick a date that already has a record, it loads that record and the button says **Update record**. Saving replaces it.
- **Cancel** (or ✕, or Esc) throws the draft away.

## Split credit cards

If you share a card, enter just your part as the amount (for example half of the balance).
