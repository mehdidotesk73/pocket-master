# Present

## Net equity

A record is a list of line items, each a name and an amount:

- **Positive** amounts are things you have: bank balances, money coming in.
- **Negative** amounts are things you owe or have committed: card balances, bills due, money going out. They're shown in parentheses: **($730.48)**.

The app sorts them for you: **net equity = total owned − total owed**.

## Saving a record

Tap **+ New record**. You get a compact list of rows, already filled in from your latest record, so you only change what moved.

- Tap **+** / **−** next to an amount to switch a row between have and owe. Owed amounts show in parentheses, like **(730.48)**; while you type, you just enter the number.
- Drag the **⠿** grip on the left of a row to move it. New records keep the order of your latest record.
- **+ Add row** adds a line; **✕** removes one.
- **Save as template** makes the current rows the starting list for every new record from now on.
- **Each record has a date and time**, set to now when you open it; change either if you're catching up. You can save as many as you like on the same day. The latest one is your live position.
- **Edit latest** opens your most recent record to change it. Older ones open from the Past tab.
- **Cancel** (or ✕, or Esc) throws the draft away.

## Split credit cards

If you share a card, enter just your part as the amount (for example half of the balance).
